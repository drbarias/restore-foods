THE PARW STUDY
This app is part of the PARW Study — research into how early we can catch and reverse unhealthy fat accumulation in adults. The study follows real patients at two New Jersey sites to see how diet changes their long-term health.
THE RESTORE PROTOCOL
RESTORE is Dr. Barias's framework for guiding patients back to metabolic health — using food, not just medication. The Open, Narrowing, and Closing Windows describe where your body stands today. This app puts that framework in your pocket.
This app provides dietary guidance only — not medical advice. Always consult your physician before making significant dietary changes.
